# ADR-0002: Launch Truth Standard and Channel Scope

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-05-04
- Owners: @5h4d0wn1k

## Context

The company vision is broad: global, end-to-end sales automation across lead generation, outreach, and conversion. The current repo and current compliance environment do not justify documenting that entire vision as already delivered.

Without an explicit truth standard, the product, marketing, roadmap, and pricing layers will drift apart.

## Decision

Adopt an operationally honest launch truth standard:

- document the long-term autonomous vision clearly
- keep day-one claims constrained to current repo truth and defensible near-term scope
- treat approval-gated automation as the default posture
- classify channels as GA, beta / guarded, roadmap, or prohibited claim

## Why

- market trust is damaged by exaggerated AI claims
- the repo already supports a reviewable, approval-aware revenue workflow better than a broad autonomy story
- compliance and provider constraints differ sharply by channel
- disciplined claims reduce legal, brand, and support risk

## Consequences

### Positive

- docs, roadmap, and public copy can stay aligned
- teams know what is safe to promise
- channel expansion becomes easier to govern

### Negative

- some ambitions must be documented as roadmap rather than current differentiators
- the public story sounds less flashy than aggressive AI-sales marketing

## Follow-up

- maintain the public-claims truth matrix
- review channel classifications whenever provider posture or repo truth changes
