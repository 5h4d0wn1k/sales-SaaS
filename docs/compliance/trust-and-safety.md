# Trust and Safety Narrative

## Product trust promise

Sales AaaS should feel safer than ad hoc sales automation because it makes risk visible:

- who the workspace is trying to reach
- why a lead was chosen
- what the campaign is trying to do
- whether approval is required
- which provider path executed
- what happened afterward

## Principles

### 1. Approval before escalation

The first or risky outbound action should remain approval-gated until the platform has earned deeper trust.

### 2. Visibility beats black-box automation

Operators should be able to see lead state, campaign state, provider mode, meeting state, and billing state without guessing.

### 3. Guardrails are product features

Suppression handling, unsubscribe posture, audit evidence, and workspace-aware control are part of the value proposition.

### 4. Claims must stay behind truth

The docs and product surface must not imply channel maturity, compliance coverage, or autonomy that the system cannot actually support.

## Trust controls that matter most

### Identity and access

- authenticated workspace access
- explicit workspace selection
- owner/admin/member boundaries
- lifecycle recovery paths such as verification and password reset

### Channel controls

- approval requirements
- provider mode visibility
- configured vs mock distinction
- prohibited-channel clarity

### Commercial controls

- plan visibility
- usage visibility
- cancellation posture
- future entitlements and AI budget enforcement

### Operational controls

- request traceability
- audit-aware sensitive flows
- rollout discipline
- rollback discipline

## Safety boundaries for marketing and sales claims

### Safe claims

- reviewable outbound operations
- approval-aware workflow
- billing and workspace visibility
- AI assistance with human control

### Unsafe claims

- hands-free global sales execution
- guaranteed compliant outreach everywhere
- unrestricted LinkedIn automation
- autonomous calling without strong qualifiers

## What "production-ready" means in this context

For Sales AaaS, production-ready does not mean every channel is automated. It means the system is honest, operable, and hard to misuse in its launch scope.
