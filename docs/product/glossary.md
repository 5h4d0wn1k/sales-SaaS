# Shared Glossary

This glossary standardizes product language across web copy, internal docs, roadmap, and future implementation work.

## Business context

### Business profile

The structured description of a customer's business, offer, website, target audience, goals, channels, regions, constraints, voice, and summary.

### ICP

Ideal customer profile. The customer type the workspace is trying to find, qualify, and convert.

### Constraints

Business, compliance, budget, brand, or operational limits that shape sourcing and outreach behavior.

## Revenue workflow

### Lead source

A source category or provider used to discover candidate leads.

### Source plan

The workspace-level plan for where leads should come from, how they will be searched, and why those sources fit the business context.

### Source run

A concrete execution of part of the source plan, including candidate counts and qualification outcomes.

### Lead

A candidate record representing a person or account that may be relevant for the customer's business goals.

### Campaign

A structured outreach motion for a set of leads, including channel, stage, approvals, and next action.

### Outreach activity

A concrete outbound action or draft tied to a campaign and lead.

### Call

A voice workflow record, including provider state, disposition, summary, transcript, and review requirement.

### Meeting

A scheduled or completed conversion event tied to a lead and owner.

### Conversion outcome

The user-visible result of moving a lead from qualification through meeting and toward won or lost status.

## Tenant and access model

### Organization

The top-level customer entity for billing and broader ownership.

### Workspace

The operating environment where one team or client context runs onboarding, lead, campaign, meeting, and billing workflow.

### Membership

The explicit relationship between a user and an organization or workspace.

### Owner / admin / member

Role levels used to control access and authority inside the tenant model.

## Trust and control

### Approval-gated action

An action that requires explicit approval before execution because of risk, novelty, or channel sensitivity.

### Risky action

An action with higher trust, compliance, cost, or brand impact, such as first outbound action, live calling, or market-sensitive dispatch.

### Audit event

A durable record that a sensitive action was attempted, approved, denied, dispatched, or mutated.

## Commercial model

### SaaS subscription

The recurring workspace subscription for software access and usage boundaries.

### AaaS managed service

The service-led operating layer that supplements the software with hands-on help, approvals, strategy, or execution support.

### AI budget

A future entitlement boundary for model spend or AI-assisted workflow usage.

### Entitlement

A feature, quota, or budget right granted by plan or commercial agreement.

### Quota

A numeric usage boundary, such as seats, lead volume, or future AI usage.

### Usage event

A trustworthy record of billable or enforceable platform consumption.
